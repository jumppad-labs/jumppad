# Gotchas

**Tier:** looked-up

**Purpose:** Sharp edges and non-obvious traps — surprising behaviours, easy-to-make mistakes, and the things that bite a contributor who does not already know about them.

**Belongs elsewhere:** Not a standing rule (that is a convention) and not the structure of the system (that is architecture). A gotcha is a warning about a specific trap and how to avoid it.

**Entry shape:** A short warning naming the trap, why it surprises, and what to do instead.
